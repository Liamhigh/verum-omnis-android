PART A — THE NINE-BRAIN ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

Verum Omnis uses a "Nine-Brain" system where analysis is distributed across
nine specialized modules. Each brain has a specific job, and no single brain can
dominate or override the others. This prevents bias and ensures thorough
examination of evidence.

  B1  Contradiction        Finds inconsistencies across statements
  B2  Document Forensics   Checks metadata, tamper indicators
  B3  Communications       Analyzes chat logs for deletions
  B4  Linguistics          Detects evasion and gaslighting patterns
  B5  Timeline/Geographic  Reconstructs event sequences
  B6  Financial            Flags hidden payments, duplicates
  B7  Legal Mapping        Maps facts to legal categories
  B8  Audio Forensics      Checks for edits and deepfakes
  B9  R&D Advisory         Suggests further checks (non-voting)

For any conclusion to be accepted, at least three independent brains must agree.
If they do not reach consensus, the output is explicitly marked as
"INSUFFICIENT" or "INDETERMINATE".

Triple Verification Doctrine (the heart of Verum Omnis). Every conclusion must
pass three independent checks before it can be considered valid:

  1. Thesis    — What the evidence appears to state. Claims and facts are
                 extracted directly from the evidence, with precise anchors
                 showing exactly where each piece of information comes from.
                 No interpretation beyond what is directly supported.

  2. Antithesis — What could contradict it. The system actively searches for
                 conflicting timestamps, documents, versions, metadata,
                 statements and gaps, and lists alternative explanations that
                 remain possible due to missing evidence.

  3. Synthesis  — What can be concluded safely. Only conclusions that remain
                 valid after contradiction checks are accepted. If synthesis
                 cannot be made safely, the output states "INSUFFICIENT".

The Golden Rule: If any leg of the triple verification fails, the conclusion is
constitutionally invalid. The system would rather say "I don't know" than
present an unverified claim.
